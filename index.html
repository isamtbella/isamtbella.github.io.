The word is a technology between worlds. For 11 years I have been weaving a new language between human subjectivity and the rigor of machines: I turn data into decisions, routines into automation and ideas into web systems, chatbots and AI, and prepare people to sustain it all.

Trained in Psychology and Systems Analysis and Development, I start from a simple idea: what is not heard keeps repeating, in people and in systems. I listen for the real pain before writing code and turn it into a tailored solution. I lead agile teams and, as an LGBTQIAPN+ and disabled woman, I invite people and institutions to co-create technology with meaning and autonomy.
